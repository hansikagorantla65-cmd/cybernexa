        HOW IT WORKS

        From message to verdict in four steps

        01

        Paste content

        Drop in a message, email, or link exactly as you received it.

        02

        Pattern matching

        The engine checks it against known scam, phishing, and social-engineering signatures.

        03

        Every match adds weighted points to a transparent 0–100 risk score.

        04

        Nothing you paste or upload is ever transmitted anywhere. The detection engine and the screenshot text-reader both run as JavaScript directly inside your browser. Images and messages are analyzed on-device and never leave this tab — no server, no account, no data collection.

        CyberShield AI uses a transparent, rule-based heuristic engine (pattern & keyword matching), not a live threat-intelligence feed. Treat every result as a helpful second opinion, not a guarantee — when in doubt, verify directly with the organization involved.
